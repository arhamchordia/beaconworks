# BeaconWorks Company Brief

BeaconWorks is a B2B SaaS company selling a support-ticket and customer-operations
platform to mid-size service businesses (logistics, insurance, manufacturing,
professional services). Founded 2021. About 29 active customer accounts across
SMB, Mid-Market and Enterprise segments in North America, EMEA and APAC.

## Current situation

- Revenue softened in month 14 of the trailing 18-month window. The cause is a
  single Enterprise account, Harbor Bridge Financial (customer ID CUST-0007),
  churning after a support escalation went unresolved for three weeks.
- Support ticket volume is up 22% quarter over quarter, concentrated in billing
  and bug tickets. Leadership wants a repeatable weekly reporting workflow
  instead of a manual pull each Friday.
- Product launch: a Priority Scoring feature for the ticket dashboard, intended
  to stop the next Harbor Bridge style churn by surfacing at-risk accounts earlier.
