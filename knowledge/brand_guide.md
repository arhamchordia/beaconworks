# BeaconWorks Brand Guide

## Voice
Direct, calm, evidence-first. BeaconWorks talks to operations leaders, not
consumers. No hype language ("revolutionary", "game-changing").

## Palette
- Primary: #1F3A5F (deep navy)
- Accent: #2FA88E (teal)
- Warning: #D9764A (warm coral, reserved for at-risk-account states)
- Neutral background: #F7F5F0 (warm white)

## Logo mark
A simple upward chevron inside a rounded square. Never stretch, never recolor
outside the palette above.

## Typography
Headings: Calibri Bold. Body: Calibri Regular.
